# Phase 0 research memo — delirium-watch

This memo is generated by `research/phase0/render_docs.py` from harness
`results.json` files. Narrative is the template; every quantitative claim is
substituted from those files. Re-run `make research` after changing a harness.

**Safety.** This project is retrospective research on de-identified or
synthetic data. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic, and not for
clinical deployment. No MIMIC-IV row is committed or processed in Phase 0.

---

## §1 Problem statement

ICU delirium is common, associated with longer stays and worse cognition, and
is still caught late. The CAM-ICU exists; what does not exist in most ICUs is
an early warning that a patient now CAM-negative will flip inside a declared
horizon (6, 12, or 24 hours) using only data available at the prediction time.

The people who pay for the miss are patients who get restrained or
antipsychotics after the fact, nurses who cannot staff a 1:1, and trials that
cannot enroll a prodrome cohort. The obvious fix — "just look at the last
CAM-ICU" — is a detection tool, not a forecast. Models trained on MIMIC
already predict delirium; most of them either leak future chart time, collapse
three incompatible labels into one, or treat nursing notes as optional color.

This repo measures those three failure modes before it trains anything.

---

## §2 Prior art

| Tool | What it does | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAM-ICU / ICDSC | Bedside detection | Not a forecast; missingness is informative |
| NEWS2 / SOFA | Severity scores | Not delirium-specific; no notes |
| MIMIC delirium classifiers (Wong, Castro, others) | Structured + sometimes notes | Label definition rarely ablated; leakage rarely audited as a failing test |
| Anticholinergic burden scales (ACB, ADS) | Drug-risk scores | High ACB without an event (P002) is the counterexample |
| CONCERN / nursing-note deterioration | NLP on notes | Notes-only cases (P003) are not a first-class hold-out |
| Commercial EWS (Epic Deterioration Index) | Closed early-warning | Not reproducible; not a research artifact |

Something already "solves" delirium prediction: several MIMIC papers report
AUROC. Another engine is still worth building because (1) CAM-ICU,
antipsychotics, and restraints do not measure the same thing, (2) post-cutoff
chart time is usually filtered in a notebook rather than rejected by a
tested window, and (3) notes-only prodromes disappear the moment notes are
ablated. The product of this repo is a leakage-safe forecast with a declared
label, not another AUROC on an undefined endpoint.

## §4 Technical selection

Each choice below is expensive to reverse. Options, criterion, measurement,
decision.

### 4.1 Label definition

Options: CAM-ICU-like assessments only; antipsychotic administration only;
restraint orders only; any-of-three union; notes-augmented gold.

Criterion: pairwise Cohen's kappa on the analysis cohort. If mean kappa
< 0.60, label choice dominates downstream results.

Measurement: `research/phase0/label_agreement/` on 49 included stays.

| pair | n | both+ | left only | right only | neither | agreement | kappa |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cam_icu vs antipsychotic | 49 | 10 | 14 | 11 | 14 | 0.490 | -0.023 |
| cam_icu vs restraint | 49 | 8 | 16 | 10 | 15 | 0.469 | -0.067 |
| antipsychotic vs restraint | 49 | 7 | 14 | 11 | 17 | 0.490 | -0.061 |

Mean pairwise kappa = -0.050.

Decision: **Label choice dominates downstream results. Mean pairwise kappa is -0.050 (< 0.60). Phase 2 must treat the three definitions as competing endpoints, not pick a silent primary.**

### 4.2 Prediction horizon

Options: 6h, 12h, 24h, or a single undeclared window.

Criterion: the horizon is a product parameter, not a tuneable after the
model is fit. Base rates at each horizon must be published before Phase 2.

Measurement: `research/phase0/base_rate/` (raw n=50, included
n=49, excluded n=1: P004).

| label | n+ | prevalence | median hours to event | rate ≤6h | rate ≤12h | rate ≤24h |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cam_icu | 24 | 0.490 | 28.9 | 0.020 | 0.020 | 0.184 |
| antipsychotic | 21 | 0.429 | 31.3 | 0.000 | 0.061 | 0.143 |
| restraint | 18 | 0.367 | 28.5 | 0.000 | 0.041 | 0.122 |

Decision: **keep 6 / 12 / 24 hours as a declared parameter.** Default
reporting horizon is 12h. CAM-ICU prevalence on the synthetic included
set is 0.490. Real MIMIC-IV prevalence is **unmeasured**.

### 4.3 Feature window and leakage

Options: SQL `WHERE charttime <= cutoff` in a notebook; a tested window
that raises; allow post-cutoff "labels as features".

Criterion: a post-cutoff event must be rejected by name and timestamp.
Non-zero exit if it appears in `used`.

Measurement: `research/phase0/leakage_audit/`. Offending P005 feature:
**heart_rate** at **2024-01-07T06:00:00** (subject P005).

| case | rejected | feature | timestamp | subject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P005 committed post-cutoff heart_rate | True | heart_rate | 2024-01-07T06:00:00 | P005 |
| P001 injected post-cutoff lactate | True | injected_post_cutoff_lactate | 2024-01-03T06:00:00 | P001 |

P003 notes ablation loses the case: true.

Decision: **`leakage_safe_window` in `src/delirium_watch/features.py` is
the only legal feature constructor.** Build this before any model.

### 4.4 Orchestration and storage

Options for orchestration: a Python function chain; a streaming feature
store; an ad-hoc notebook.

Criterion: every window independently unit-testable; typed events; no
hidden globals. Latency of a feature store vs a function is **unmeasured**.

Decision: **function chain in Phase 0/2.** A store is Phase 3 if batch
MIMIC replay needs it.

Options for storage: JSON tables; DuckDB; Parquet.

Criterion: local, queryable, no service. DuckDB is the repo convention.

Decision: **JSON for the committed cohort, DuckDB views for harness
queries.** DuckDB vs Parquet scan time on full MIMIC is **unmeasured**.

## §5 Evaluation design

Hold-out (Phase 3, not run here): a credentialed MIMIC-IV extract with a
pinned split manifest, seed 0, no reshuffle in CI. Until then, numbers
are synthetic.

Metrics, declared before any model is built:

- AUPRC (primary; class imbalance makes AUROC secondary)
- Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, reliability diagrams
- Horizon-specific AUPRC at 6 / 12 / 24 hours
- Subgroup AUPRC: age ≥65, sex, ventilated, first 48h of stay
- Alert burden: alarms per 100 patient-days and PPV at two operating points
- Notes-ablation delta (P003 is the teaching case)
- Baseline: last CAM-ICU carried forward
- Second baseline: anticholinergic-burden threshold (P002 is the counterexample)
- Third baseline: NEWS2-like vital score without notes

Split: pinned, committed split manifest. Seed 0. No random reshuffle in CI.

**Failure condition (written before the answer):** the project fails if, on
the credentialed hold-out, the full system does not beat the last-CAM and
ACB baselines on 12h AUPRC by at least 0.05, **or** if a notes-ablated
run still claims P003-type cases, **or** if any feature used at time t has
timestamp > t, **or** if results are reported on only one of the three
labels after this memo recorded disagreement.

Phase 0 does not fit a model. Model AUPRC, Brier, subgroup, and
alert-burden are **unmeasured**. The measurement that would settle them
is the Phase 2/3 eval harness on the pinned split.

## §6 Cost and scale model

Phase 0 is local CPU: 50 synthetic stays and three deterministic
harnesses. Numbers:

- Synthetic cohort generation: seed 0, n=50, no network
- DuckDB in-memory views over the JSON tables
- Live PhysioNet download size: **unmeasured** (full MIMIC-IV + notes is
  tens to hundreds of GB; the measurement is a timed `wget` after
  credentialing)
- GPU training time: **unmeasured**
- Note-embedding API cost: **unmeasured** (Phase 2 may stay lexical)
- PhysioNet review wait for a specific applicant: **unmeasured**
  (documented range in `docs/DATA.md` is weeks)

Unmeasured: human chart-review time saved, ICU length-of-stay impact.
This project will not claim clinical impact in Phase 0–3.

## §7 Risks

| Risk | Type | Mitigation or acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synthetic kappa ≠ MIMIC kappa | Data | Accepted for Phase 0. Memo says so. Credentialed extract retires it. |
| Antipsychotics given for psychiatric disease, not delirium | Data | Keep the label; do not silently recode indication. |
| Comfort-care leakage into the training set | Technical | P004. `is_excluded` is a failing test if it returns false. |
| Post-cutoff vitals copied forward | Technical | leakage_audit exits non-zero if `used` contains them. |
| Output read as a bedside monitor | Legal | Disclaimer on CLI, README, memo. Not a device. Not for deployment. |
| Notes contain residual identifiers in real MIMIC | Legal | Phase 0 uses synthetic notes only. Real notes stay behind the DUA. |
| AUROC shopping across horizons | Scope | Horizon is a parameter declared here. |

1. Whether a credentialed MIMIC-IV extract reproduces the low pairwise
   kappa measured on this synthetic cohort (real kappa **unmeasured**).

   P003 is a population, not a singleton.

   plots after exclusions.
